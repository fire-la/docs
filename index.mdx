## Why FIREla?

Most finance apps own your data. With **FIREla**, you own your data.

- **Beancount-based** - Plain text accounting you can audit
- **One book for life** - Across regions, banks, and life stages
- **Zero tracking** - The cloud sees the ledger, never you
- **No vendor lock-in** - Export anytime, migrate anywhere

## Get Started

The Lifetime Ledger app is in active development. Today you can [deploy the collector](/quickstart) to Cloudflare Workers, or keep your books in any Beancount-compatible tool — your data is plain text either way.
