# Introducing Firela

Most financial apps have a dirty secret: your history lives in their database. Even where export exists, it gives you CSV rows — not a ledger that outlives the app. And inspecting what the app actually does with your data is rarely possible.

**Firela is a beancount lifetime ledger — a ledger for life.**

## Why We Built This

We believe your financial data is among your most personal information. It reveals:

- Where you shop and what you buy
- How much you earn and save
- Your habits, goals, and anxieties

This data shouldn't be held in a format that serves the platform more than it serves you.

## Where the Project Stands

- **billclaw, the collector** — deployable now to Cloudflare Workers' free tier ([quickstart](/quickstart))
- **The mobile app** — in active development (not yet on app stores); its parser and sanitizer libraries are public today
- **The ledger** — complete Beancount semantics, plain text, yours for life

## Get Involved
